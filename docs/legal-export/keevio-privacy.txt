<h2>2. Overview – What does Keevio do?</h2>
<p>
    Keevio is an AI-powered application that analyzes YouTube videos and transforms their content into 
    <strong>clear, structured, and actionable insights</strong>.
</p>
<p>
    Users paste a YouTube link, and Keevio automatically extracts key ideas, summaries, and core takeaways.
</p>
<p>
    The goal of Keevio is to make valuable knowledge accessible <strong>in minutes instead of hours</strong>.
</p>
